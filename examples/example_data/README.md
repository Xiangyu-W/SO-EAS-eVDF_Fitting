# Example data

Two fitting-ready samples, one per pipeline, plus the calibration tables both
need. Nothing here requires a download.

## Poisson pipeline: `poisson_2023-04-04/`

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `VDF_eas_forFitting_20230404005008_20230404011908_1min.h5` | 30 one-minute epochs of preprocessed SWA-EAS electron VDF data, 2023-04-04 00:50:08-01:19:08 UT (47 MB) |
| `VDF_eas_forFitting_..._two_population_crossing.csv` | break energies from the two-population crossing method (input to the fit; regenerated by notebook 03) |

**Provenance.** Solar Orbiter SWA-EAS (EAS1+EAS2) normal-mode 3D
measurements at 0.33 au, six days before perihelion: SOAR L2 PSD V03 and L1
counts V03 files, preprocessed with `src/preprocess_data_for_fit_hdf5.py`
exactly as shown in notebook 02 (MAG-SRF field-aligned rotation, PAS and
RPW density from CDAWeb, 1-minute resampling, L1 saturation blocks masked).
The spacecraft potential is the SOAR RPW L3 `rpw-bia-scpot` product
(BICAS 9.4.0, generated 2026-07-30), reduced to the 20 s table
`scpot_rpw_l3_20230404_20230405_20s.csv` by `src/build_scpot_table_from_soar_l3.py`.
The file holds epochs 50-79 of the six-hour segment
`VDF_eas_forFitting_20230404000008_20230404060008_1min.h5`: 27 epochs carry a strong anti-parallel strahl, 2 a weak one and 1 get no strahl verdict (the no-strahl branch of the fit is exercised too); all have a valid crossing break energy; 4 epochs carry masked L1 saturation blocks below the energy cut. The demonstration epoch of the notebooks
is epoch 20 (01:10:08 UT). The full two days (2 850 epochs, eight files,
4.8 GB) are distributed as a GitHub release; see `workshop/README.md`. The
reference fit of the subset is `results/fitting/example_run_poisson/`, that
of the two days `results/fitting/example_days_2023-04-04_05/summary.csv`.

## Legacy chi-square pipeline: the 2022-03-02 sample

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `VDF_eas_forFitting_20220302121509_20220302122509_1min.h5` | 11 one-minute epochs, 2022-03-02 12:15:09-12:25:09 UT at ~0.55 au (~20 MB) |
| `VDF_eas_forFitting_..._cone_feature_split.pkl` / `.csv` | break energies from the cone-split method (input to the legacy pipeline) |

Produced from the MSSL corrected L2 PSD (`corrLPP_V01`) and L1 counts with
`shift_SCPOT=True` (RPW potential from CDAWeb), epochs 10-20 of the 12:05-18:05 UT
segment. The cone-split files keep the **full six-hour series** (361 rows):
the legacy fitting smooths the break-energy series over all rows before the
nearest-timestamp lookup, so shipping the complete series keeps the example
fits bit-identical to a run on the full segment. `python examples/run_example.py`
fits it; the structure is documented in [docs/TUTORIAL.md](../../docs/TUTORIAL.md).

## Calibration and ancillary tables

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `eas_head_geometry.npz` | elevation and azimuth centres and widths, energy table and `EASx_TO_SRF` rotation of both heads, extracted from one L2 file by `src/eas_geometry.py` (notebook 01) |
| `scpot_rpw_l3_20230404_20230405_20s.csv` | spacecraft potential, 20 s medians of the SOAR RPW L3 daily files (QUALITY_FLAG 3), columns `time, scpotV, scpotSigmaV, nSamples, qualityFlagMin` |
| `EAS1/2_AGFs_*.txt`, `EAS1/2_Flight_QuantumEfficiencies_*.line` | EAS geometric factors and quantum efficiencies: counts to PSD and the VDF uncertainty in the preprocessing |
| `energy_deltas.npz` | EAS energy bin widths, used by the legacy fitting for the strahl moment calculation |
